COORDINATES
Columns are letters $first_col-$last_col from left to right. Rows are numbers 1-$size from bottom to top. A point is written as column letter followed by row number, for example $example. The centre point is $centre.

BOARD FORMAT
"." is an empty point, "X" is a black stone, "O" is a white stone. The stone played on the previous turn is shown in lowercase.
